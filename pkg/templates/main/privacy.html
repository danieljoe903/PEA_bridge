YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Flexy Properties respects the privacy of users, property seekers, property owners and authorized representatives who use our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, how that information may be used, and how information submitted through features such as account registration, property enquiries and property submissions may be handled.

1. Information We May Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Flexy Properties and the features you choose to use. This information may include:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone or WhatsApp number, where provided.
  • Profile photograph, where uploaded.
  • Account and authentication information necessary to manage your account.
  • Messages, enquiries or other information submitted through website forms.
  • Information relating to properties in which you express interest.
  • Property information submitted through the Market Your Property feature.
  • Technical information reasonably necessary for website operation, security and troubleshooting.

2. Property Submission Information

Registered users may use the Market Your Property feature to submit a property to Flexy Properties for review and possible marketing.

When you submit a property, we may collect information including:

  • Your full name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • The property title and description.
  • Property type, such as house, apartment, land or commercial property.
  • Whether the property is being offered for sale or rent.
  • The property address, state and other location information you provide.
  • The asking sale or rental price.
  • Photographs or other property-related materials uploaded with the submission.
  • The unique reference number associated with the submission.
Property Submission Notice: Information submitted through the Market Your Property feature is provided to Flexy Properties for review. Submitting a property does not automatically publish the property on the website or guarantee that Flexy Properties will accept it for marketing.

3. How We May Use Your Information

Information collected through Flexy Properties may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account security.
  • Provide property search and account functionality.
  • Process and manage property interest requests.
  • Respond to property enquiries and other communications.
  • Receive, organize and review property submissions.
  • Contact a property submitter where clarification, additional information or verification is required.
  • Determine whether a submitted property may be suitable for publication and marketing through Flexy Properties.
  • Prepare approved property information for publication on the website.
  • Send transactional emails relating to accounts, property interests and property submissions.
  • Provide and improve website functionality.
  • Detect, investigate and prevent misuse, fraud, unauthorized access and security incidents.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of Flexy Properties.

4. Property Photographs and Submitted Content

Property owners or authorized representatives may upload photographs and property information when submitting a property for review.

These materials may be stored and reviewed by Flexy Properties for the purpose of evaluating the submission and, where the property is accepted, preparing and managing the property's listing.

Users should only upload photographs, descriptions and other materials that they are authorized to provide to Flexy Properties.

5. Information That May Become Public

Property submissions are not automatically displayed publicly.

If Flexy Properties approves a property for publication, selected property information may become publicly visible as part of the property listing. This may include:

  • Property title.
  • Property description.
  • Property type.
  • Sale or rental status.
  • Price.
  • General location information.
  • Property photographs.
  • Other property details considered appropriate for the listing.

Personal contact information provided by a property submitter is not intended to be automatically displayed as part of a public property listing unless there is an appropriate reason and authorization to do so.

6. Email Communications

Flexy Properties may use the email address you provide to send service-related and transactional communications.

These communications may include:

  • Password reset communications.

  • Property submission review or status communications.
  • Responses to enquiries or support requests.
